## Patterns

Sculptor-name split: मह्लाचार्य- 4× on p. 2 of the range (F3–F6: मह्लाचार्यस्य, मह्लाचार्यवर्य, मह्लाचार्यः, मह्लाचार्येण) against मल्लाचार्य- 7× on pp. 3–4 (मल्लाचार्यम्, मल्लाचार्यः ×3, मल्लाचार्येण, मल्लाचार्यद्वारा ×2); one person throughout (Avanti sculptor commissioned by letter, delivers statue, is summoned). Held as candidates toward मल्ला- (real word, majority) because the p. 2 glyph reading itself is only medium-confidence and the direction must survive a print check.

F3–F6: मह्ला- vs मल्ला- direction and the p. 2 glyph reading itself — settles by reopening scan 3. F7: क्षमतु vs क्षम्यताम् (passive) — settles by dictionary/usage check plus print re-check of the आ matra. F9: कर्मचराः vs कर्मकराः glyph (च vs क) — settles by print re-check. U1 (no block): p002:P4 रुद्राक्षः आत्मानं, `काषायवर्णस्य पीताम्बराणि` — genitive काषायवर्णस्य stands unconnected (ochre vs yellow also clash); possibly misread वर्णानि or a print slip, undecidable from text — settles by print re-check only.
