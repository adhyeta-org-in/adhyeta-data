## Unverified

- F1 `आस्थानकविः पत्नी`: tried apposition (कविः + पत्नी as co-subjects — verb कृतवती is fem. sg., so no), vocative (no addressee in narration), and one-word-compound आस्थानकविपत्नी (print has a visarga, so this needs two changes, not one); genitive कवेः is the unique fit and matches कवेः पत्नी (p03:P10) and कवेः पुरतः (p03:P8). Would settle: 3x re-read of the े/ि mātrā on file — images stay closed, so this goes to the user.
- F2 `अयच्छन्`: tried शतृ-participle reading यच्छन् (participles take no augment अ), implied-plural subject (the तु-contrast and sg. अनुसृतवान् pin मन्त्री alone), and किमपि-as-negation (किमपि is indefinite "some", and no न is printed, so "gave some reply" strands with no quoted reply). Would settle: 3x re-read of त/न्; if print truly reads न्, sense emendation is the user's call.
- F3 `उष्णजलशाखेन`: tried शाखा as "sprinkler-twig" and as tubing/pipe branch (both strained nonce compounds), and शोथेन/स्नानेन (further from the ductus than सेकेन). Would settle: dictionary check of शाखा in a medical sense plus print re-check — both beyond this phase, so held.
