## Patterns

- Quote-mark drops and sort slips at इति closures (F6, F7, F8): the printer loses the opening double of a quoted vision, prints a single for a double open, and drops an inner close, each exactly where a speech or vision meets इति. Counted once here with three locations; no other instances found.

## Unverified

- F3 आरुढवान् versus आरूढवान्: short रु read at 5x in Phase A, but u-length at this print size is marginal; a second print copy would settle it.
- F9 कुतूहलः versus कुतूहलम्: masculine internally consistent, but lexicon gender unchecked against a dictionary; settle with Apte or MW.
- F10 निस्साहेन versus निरुत्साहेन: sense strongly favours निरुत्साहेन, but the glyph cannot be rechecked (Phase B, images closed); settle against another copy.
- F11 उपहासपात्रिः versus उपहासपात्रम्: sense strongly favours पात्रम्, but त्रिः versus त्रम् cannot be rechecked; settle against another copy.
