## Patterns

- Single-open/double-close mismatches (5): F2, F5, F8, F10, F11. Print twice doubles a single close and twice singles a double open; each resolved by speech-vs-thought sense (speech doubles, thought singles).
- Stray Latin full stops (3): F4, F7, F9. Each deleted; no other Latin punctuation remains in the story.
- House spellings kept, not findings: open/joined sandhi mixed throughout (तत् तु vs तत्सर्वम्, तच्च); ह्यपूर्वकः read as हि + अपूर्वकः; आसम् as Epic 1sg; तद्गुल्मानां, दुरदृष्टं, तत्रत्याः, व्याममिताम्, अयस्किट्टेन all grammatical as printed; आगत as participle agreeing with the subject.
