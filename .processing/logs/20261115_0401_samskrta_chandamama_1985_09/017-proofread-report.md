## Patterns

- Short quoted questions/addresses open `‘` and close `’"` (inner `’` + outer `"`) at 6 sites: `‘इदानीम्…?` (p17:P4), `‘तात…` (p18:P4), `‘महाप्रभो…` (p19:P2), `‘महास्वामिन्…` (p20:P3), `‘अस्माकं…?` (p22:P3); at p21:P3 the same inner pair sits nested inside `"तात…"` (`"तात…‘महिमावान्…सन्ति’"`), which validates the sort. The five outer `"` marks have no pinnable mate, so all are kept as seen; no blocks.
- Mixed open/joined sandhi is house style, no action: `तत् वचनं` vs `तद्विषये`, `एतत् कार्यद्वयं` vs `तदर्थम्`, `यत् किमपि` vs `यदा`.
- `इदानीम्` (3x, all p17) vs `इदानीं` (6x, pp18–22): compositor wobble, kept as seen, no action.
