## Patterns

- Quote-sort normalization (7x: F1-F7): print mixes single opens with double closes and vice versa; each span ruled by its reporting verb (उक्तवान्/उक्त्वा/पृष्टवान् → doubles; निश्चितवान्/sense-memory with no speech verb → singles).
- Surplus strokes after dialogue-dash opens (3x: F8-F10): a colon-like double dot (p011) and two single strokes (p014, p015) printed immediately after a certain `"` open; all deleted.

## Unverified

- U1 p015:P5 (विश्वासार्हान् धैर्यसाहसशीलान्) पञ्चषान्*: opaque word kept as printed with an inline x-com; no certain replacement (पञ्चाशतं, पञ्चजनान्, or a proper noun all conceivable). Settles with a lexicon check plus a higher-resolution re-read of ष vs श/स.
- U2 p015:P5 (राज्यसीमानन्तरं पश्चिमखातस्य) पश्चिमखातस्य*: खात (pit) parses as a frontier place-name, but खण्डस्य (division) fits "before entering the western ___" better. Settles with a 3x re-read of त vs ण्ड; no block placed since Phase A logged no glyph doubt.
- U3 p017:P5 (तदा तेन तत्र) कश्चन शिलाफलकं*: masculine pronoun with neuter noun; classical Sanskrit wants किञ्चन, but the change is too large for a slip so this reads as house usage. Settles with a cross-issue consistency check of कश्चन with neuter nouns.
