# Chandamama proofread (Phase B, images closed)

Second phase on the same story range, by the same agent that transcribed it: the eyes that read the glyphs now rule on them from text alone. Output is the same story file amended with verdicts and candidates, plus a findings report. Images stay closed for the whole phase — glyph questions were settled in Phase A; anything Phase A left uncertain is ruled here from grammar, house style, and story context, or held as a candidate for the user. Nothing is ever fixed silently, and nothing goes back for another look.

## Input and output

- Story file `.sources/data/<ISSUE>/p<START>.md` as Phase A left it: passing the extract gate. The report path `.logs/<RUN>/<START>-proofread-report.md` (`<START>` = story start page, 3 digits; `000` for prelude) — both given by the coordinator, which created `.logs/<RUN>/` once per issue run.
- The same story file amended: every routing lands as a block at its span. Story text is otherwise byte-identical.
- Format reference: `.agents/refs/chandamama/1984_04/p01.proofread.md` — verdicts (`{del:}`/`{ins:}`) promoted from print, the supplied `{ins:"}`, the kept `{sug-ins:यदा सोमः}` candidate. Compare against `p01.extract.md` to see exactly what Phase B may change (blocks only) and what it must not (text).
- Report file written directly by this agent at the given path (never anywhere else), incrementally as rulings land — a crash must lose at most the current paragraph.

## Doctrine

1. **Print fidelity over classical perfection.** Chandamama print mixes open and joined sandhi (`तद् दिने` vs `तद्दिने`) and keeps house spellings; record these as house-style variants, never flag them. Verdicts judge fidelity to print, not classical correctness.
2. **Report, don't rewrite.** Every correction lands as a `{del:}`/`{ins:}` block with a text-pinned span; anything unsure stays `{sug-del:}`/`{sug-ins:}`. Nothing is ever fixed silently.
3. **Examine every `{sug-ins:}`/`{sug-del:}` inherited from Phase A, then route it.** Promote to plain text (verified in print from context) or to `{del:}`/`{ins:}` (verified correction), drop with reason, or keep it as `{sug-ins:}`/`{sug-del:}` when the evidence cannot settle it — a kept suggestion is a low-confidence candidate for human review, not an open task.

## Blocks (closed vocabulary lives in `.agents/refs/chandamama-blocks.md`)

- Proofreading emits verdicts (`{del:}`/`{ins:}`) and candidates (`{sug-del:}`/`{sug-ins:}`). Structural, `x-missing:`/`x-illegible:`, and metadata blocks are read-only here — proofread node contents but never strip the blocks. `{x-com:}` may carry user-facing commentary.
- No legitimate Phase A file contains verdicts (the extract gate rejects them); any `{del:}`/`{ins:}` already present is contamination — re-derive it from mark, position, AND sort, adopt with full justification, correct, or drop it.

## Steps

1. Confirm the story gate passes in this phase (`python3 .agents/tools/chandamama/lint.py --phase proofread --story <story.md>`). Fix mechanical failures that are text-obvious (spacing, block syntax, NFC) directly. Anything glyph-suspect is decided here from text — images never reopen; there is no re-check channel, only rulings and held candidates.
2. After amending, re-run the proofread gate; every error fixed, every note dispositioned (promote to a finding with a block, or record as checked print-faithful with reason in the report). A green gate with unreviewed notes is not done. The proofread gate fails any para that ends mid-page with odd quotes (edge-spanning pairs stay notes-only): no unbalanced para ships.
3. Work the `{sug-ins:}`/`{sug-del:}` queue oldest first; promote, drop, or keep held, each with reason. Before holding any word for the user, widen the window: test clause-level re-segmentations against story context and record the tried hypotheses in Why — `neither fits' at word level is a verdict on the window, not on the word; holding is the last resort after text exhaustion, not the first.
4. Run `.agents/tools/sanskrit/scan.py orth` (stdlib only) on deblocked story text — strip blocks with the linter's deblock rule first:
   `python3 -c "import sys; sys.path.insert(0,'.agents/tools/chandamama'); from lint import deblock,split_story_pages; pages,_=split_story_pages('<story>'); print('\n'.join(deblock(c['text']) for _,chs in pages for c in chs))" > /tmp/<START>.deblocked.txt`
   then `python3 .agents/tools/sanskrit/scan.py orth /tmp/<START>.deblocked.txt`. Treat every hit as a hypothesis to confirm in context, never a verdict.
5. Word-level, sandhi, morphology, and syntax passes per `.agents/refs/sanskrit/prose-checks.md` (confusion pairs in `ocr-and-orthography.md`, category codes and severity in `error-taxonomy.md`, verse only via `verse.md`), with Chandamama overrides: proper nouns must spell consistently across the whole story; print quirks and archaic or Epic forms stand as seen (variants at most); apparent misprints need a Phase A `{x-com:}` or `{sug-…}` already on file before any block — Phase B invents no new glyph claims.
6. Quote pass: re-derive every inherited quote block (never ratify on sight). The gate checks balance, not sort: map every mark to its role (open / close / inner) from nesting plus speech-vs-thought sense. A `"` mark inside an already-open `"` span is suspect by default (inner speech or thought takes `'`: embedded `यत्...इति`, questions inside speech, reported plans); only a genuine close-plus-reopen justifies it. Supply missing quote marks or correct sorts via `{del:}`/`{ins:}` wherever speech-vs-thought sense plus sentence boundary pins them (reporting verbs, `यत्...इति`, iti-closures) — modern readability outranks preserving the print's punctuation gaps; keep print's marks wherever sense doesn't decide, and document for the compiler. Multi-para speech stands alone per para: map the whole speech first, then assign every mark its role — every paragraph opens and closes its own pair, supplying missing marks via `{ins:}` (page-turn continuation via `{pb}`/`{pc}` is the only exception and is never forced closed). Verify multi-para spans against the indent chain; pair tale quotes across pages.
7. Flow and reconstruction pass: walk every `J<n>` joint in the file's `## Joints` index in order — asserted `{pb}`/`{wb}` joins and sentence continuity at unmarked seams. A `{pb}` joint inside one page section is a column turn; a joint crossing sections is a page turn (closed by `{pgb}`) (the 1/20 column-order swap hides in unmarked seams; Phase A's `{x-com:layout: …}` line is the asserted model to check flow against). Rupture procedure: when continuity breaks, fit-test both orders against Sanskrit governance (case→verb, preverb→verb, `यत्`→`तत्`, sandhi junctions, agreement); unique grammatical fit → propose the move in the report (Moves section) for user decision; repeated chunk → `{del:}` verdict; rupture with no fit → suspected omission held for the user; both orders grammatical → narrative-suspicion flag (escalate if strong, note if weak). Never relitigate a clean asserted join; never issue a confident verdict on a boundary-coincident seam from text alone. Pages whose asserted layout you doubt get layout-unverified treatment: joints are still continuity-checked, but a grammatically clean seam is explicitly NOT evidence of correct order — disposition such joints `: ok-text` (text-continuous, layout unverified), never bare `: ok`, and hold genuine suspicion (not certainty) for the user. Then read the whole story straight through sentence by sentence for continuity — every sentence must follow its predecessor in subject, pronoun reference, tense, and event order — applying the rupture procedure at ANY seam, not only `J<n>` joints.
8. Report findings per `.agents/refs/sanskrit/report-format.md`, with Chandamama deltas: no corrected-text section ever (corrections travel as verdict blocks); held candidates are `unverified` (append `*` to Conf); `Loc` is keyed on story coordinates `pNN:Pm` (NN = scan sequence: filename `<START>` plus `{pgb}`-span ordinal; Pm = chunk number within that span, matching lint `P` numbers) plus the first two words as anchor, counting words on the chunk from 1 — never on scratch line numbers. A 3–5 line `## Summary`, the findings table, `## Patterns` (only if repeats), an `## Unverified` section for held items, then the required coverage appendix: one disposition line per Devanagari body chunk in file order (`pNN:Pm <first two words> : clean` / `: F<k>...`, or `: skip <reason>` for title/metadata/`x-com:`-only chunks, each written only after reading every sentence of that chunk for continuity), plus one `J<n> <edge> : ok` / `: <finding>` line per joint from the in-file `## Joints` index, verified (chunk counts and anchors match, every joint dispositioned, every finding appears in at least one line). Ship the report; the coordinator compiles on user acceptance.

## Do not

- Do not rewrite story text; blocks only — except text-obvious mechanical fixes (spacing, block syntax, NFC).
- Do not normalize sandhi openness, house spellings, or archaisms; log variants as such. Quote marks serve modern readability, not print record: supply missing marks and correct sorts wherever sense is certain; keep print's marks wherever sense doesn't decide.
- Do not invent pairings for genuine print mismatches; keep plus document.
- Do not strip `{pb}`, `{pc}`, `{wb}`, or `{pgb}`; do not touch other ranges; do not emit SXML or summaries.
- Do not open scan images, not even to "double-check". Decide from text, house rules, and story sense.
- Do not report more than about 40 findings for long stories: fold repeats into patterns with counts and example locations.
- Do not hold rulings only in context; report and log lines go to disk as you rule.
